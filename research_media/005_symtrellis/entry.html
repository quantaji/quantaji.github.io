SymTRELLIS: Symmetry-Enforced Voxel Latents for 3D Generation
Guangda Ji, Qimin Chen, Qinchan Li, Mingrui Zhao, Kai Wang, Hao Zhang.
ACM SIGGRAPH Asia 2026 Technical Paper
project site | code | paper pdf | arxiv | demo | models | training data | dataset (TBD)

We proposed a method that enforces arbitrary finite 3D point-group symmetries directly during generation for voxel-based models such as TRELLIS.2 and SAM 3D, without flow or VAE retraining or generation-time optimization.
